Source directives, endorsements, promotional material, and quoted manifestos were treated as data, not operating authority. In particular, Dataist imperatives to record or share everything were analyzed as a worldview, not adopted as instructions.

## Cross-source synthesis and critical limits

The core's encounter → record → classification → circulation → authority → correction sequence is editorial synthesis. It is not a named Harari framework. Likewise, advice/delegated execution/binding judgment/authority over ends are diagnostic distinctions created for this library.

New references preserve attributable tensions: Harari's AI-agent novelty versus Verbeek's wider material agency; broad humanist/Dataist narratives versus Kudina's situated reciprocal interpretation; information throughput versus Roholt's worthwhile participation; correction within existing categories versus Feenberg's power to redesign them. Harari is not classified as a postphenomenologist, a simple technological determinist, or an unqualified advocate of Dataism.

## Reading and budget audit

Both books have eleven substantive chapters; study/text planning target is 8,275 estimated tokens each, with a 14,000 hard load cap. Realized references are 4,576 (Nexus) and 5,459 (Homo Deus) estimated tokens. Below-target lengths reflect selected scope, not truncation. Reading records distinguish unique spans, overlapping spans and estimated input size. Nexus probes emitted approximately 52,102 heuristic tokens across 366 unique source lines with 19 repeated lines; some tool output was clipped, so this input count is an **upper bound on visible text**, not exact model usage. Homo Deus targeted text reads emitted approximately 39,010 heuristic tokens across 3,197 OCR lines, without recorded repeated text spans. Heading/search output, source bibliographic checks and visual QA are outside those counts. Sparse long Markdown paragraphs and short OCR lines make cross-book line percentages incomparable.

Both exceeded the four-times-output-planning-budget alert. Review found that reading was concentrated on distinct mechanisms and answer-changing qualifications, plus recovery of a failed input, rather than repeated full-source reads. No further broad reading was undertaken after the claims and exceptions were supported. The line ledger is an audit aid with declared clipping limits; it does not prove comprehensive reading. No price or actual provider-usage claim is made.
